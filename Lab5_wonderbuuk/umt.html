About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, providing facilities for practical training and research in marine science and fisheries.

Over the decades, the institution evolved from Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) into Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001, before officially receiving full university status and adopting its current name, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, on 1 February 2007.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier university focused on marine science, aquatic resources, coastal management, and sustainable development, attracting students and researchers from across the globe.
